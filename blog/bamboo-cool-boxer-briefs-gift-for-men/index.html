    • Sizing: The listing offers sizes from Small up to 5XL, with a waist chart (for example, M is 32–34 inches and L is 36–38 inches). Check his waist size against the chart rather than guessing, and note that not every size may be available in every color assortment or pack count.
    • Fabric care: The listing says machine wash. The brand's product description adds to wash similar colors together, machine wash at low temperature and tumble dry with low heat. Following that should help the fabric keep its shape.
