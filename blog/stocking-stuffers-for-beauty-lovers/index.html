Stocking Stuffers for Beauty Lovers (Under $25)

Beauty · October 19, 2026 · 4 min read

Stocking stuffers are where beauty gifts shine. They're small, they're fun to unwrap, and the right ones get used every single day. A beauty lover will notice the difference between a random sample and a product she actually wants, so a little thought goes a long way.

This guide covers the best beauty stocking stuffers under $25, organized by type, plus a quick formula for building a stocking she'll love. For general stocking ideas, see our stocking stuffers under $15 guide and our last-minute stocking stuffer strategy.

The beauty stocking formula

Instead of filling a stocking with five lip glosses, mix categories. A balanced beauty stocking looks like this:

  • One lip product: the most-used beauty item there is.
  • One skincare treat: a mask or a small treatment.
  • One self-care accessory: something for sleep or relaxation.
  • One scent or body item: if you know her taste.
  • One fun extra: something playful, like a bath bomb or a cozy pair of socks.

Mixing categories means every item feels different, and she won't end up with duplicates of what she already owns.

Lip products: the stocking MVP

If you buy only one beauty stocking stuffer, make it a lip product. They're small, easy to use and lip care is something almost everyone uses in winter.

  • Summer Fridays Lip Butter Balm: a cult-favorite balm with a devoted following.
  • LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask: an overnight lip treatment that feels like a treat.
  • Burt's Bees Beeswax Lip Balm: a classic that's never wasted and easy to split between several stockings.

Tip: Lip balms are one of the few beauty gifts where doubling up is fine. Tuck one in her stocking and one in her coat pocket.

Skincare treats

Small skincare gifts work best when they're gentle and easy to add to a routine. The LANEIGE Water Sleeping Mask is a popular overnight mask that layers on top of whatever she already uses. If she's a vitamin C fan, the TruSkin Vitamin C Serum is a well-known option, but only if it's on her list; actives are personal.

For hands that get dry and cracked in winter, the Burt's Bees Hand Repair Gift Set from our Secret Santa gifts under $30 guide is a cute, practical choice.

Makeup must-haves

Makeup is trickier because of shades, but some products work for almost everyone. A primer is one of them: the e.l.f. Power Grip Primer is a practical pick for anyone who wears makeup most days. Steer clear of foundations and concealers unless you know her exact shade.

Self-care and beauty sleep

Self-care accessories make excellent stocking stuffers because they feel indulgent without being something she has to "use up."

  • Kitsch Weighted Satin Sleep Mask: a soft, weighted mask for winding down.
  • Rose Quartz Roller & Gua Sha Set: a pretty facial tool set for a calming nighttime ritual.
  • Kitsch Satin Pillowcase with Zipper: folds small enough to fit in many stockings and makes a great beauty-sleep gift.

For more relaxation ideas, browse our full self-care and spa gifts guide.

Scent and body care

When you know she likes a certain kind of scent, body products are a wonderful small gift. The Sol de Janeiro Brazilian Bum Bum Cream in the smaller size is a popular stocking pick, and it shows up in our gifts for teens guide too, so it works for teen beauty lovers as well. A body mist like the Sol de Janeiro Cheirosa Perfume Mist is lighter and less of a commitment than a full perfume.

Fun extras

Every stocking needs something playful. Bath bombs are a crowd-pleaser: the LifeAround2Angels Bath Bombs Gift Set can be split across several stockings, which is handy if you're shopping for sisters, friends or coworkers. Add a pair of TEHOOK Fuzzy Socks for a cozy touch that rounds out the theme.

Beauty stocking stuffers by recipient

  • For mom: a lip mask, a hand cream set and a weighted sleep mask. Pair with something from our gifts for mom guide.
  • For teens: a lip balm, a body cream and bath bombs.
  • For the skincare obsessed: an overnight face mask and a gua sha set. See our gifts for skincare lovers for bigger ideas.
  • For the makeup lover: a primer and a lip product.
  • For coworkers or gift swaps: individual bath bombs or a classic lip balm.

Quick checklist

  • Mix categories so nothing feels repetitive.
  • Choose lip care and self-care when you're unsure of her preferences.
  • Avoid shade-specific makeup and strong actives.
  • Skip heavily scented items unless you know she likes them.
  • Check current details and sizes on Amazon before ordering.
