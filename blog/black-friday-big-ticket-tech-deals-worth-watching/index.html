Black Friday is when big-ticket gifts finally feel within reach. One honest note before we begin: deals vary. Discounts change from day to day, from retailer to retailer, and sometimes from hour to hour. We don't list prices or promise specific discounts here. Instead, we focus on who each product suits and how to tell a good deal from a noisy one.

How to judge a big-ticket Black Friday deal

Before diving into products, a few rules that apply to everything on this list:

  • Know the normal price first. Check what a product usually sells for a few weeks before Black Friday, so a "sale" label means something.
  • Compare the exact model. Storage size, screen size and connectivity options all change the value. Make sure the deal is for the version you actually want.
  • Check the seller. On Amazon, look at who ships and sells the item, and read the return policy, especially for TVs and other large items.
  • Set a budget per person. A big deal on the wrong gift is still the wrong gift. Our Black Friday shopping game plan walks through this step by step.

The big screen: LG 65" B5 Series OLED 4K Smart TV

TVs are the classic Black Friday purchase, and the LG 65" B5 Series OLED 4K Smart TV is the biggest-ticket item on our list. An OLED TV is a meaningful family gift or a major living room upgrade for movie and sports fans.

A few TV-specific tips:

  • Measure the space first. A 65-inch screen is large. Check the wall or stand where it will go, and think about viewing distance.

When to buy, and when to wait

Holiday sales tend to start before Black Friday itself and continue through Cyber Monday, but timing and discounts vary by retailer and product. A few strategies help:

  • Make a short list now. Decide which one or two big-ticket gifts you're really shopping for.
  • Check regularly. Look at your shortlisted products a few times during the sale period rather than buying the first discount you see.
  • Don't panic-buy. If a deal disappears, another may appear. If it doesn't, stick to your budget.
  • Mind gift returns. Check return windows on holiday purchases, especially if you're buying early.
