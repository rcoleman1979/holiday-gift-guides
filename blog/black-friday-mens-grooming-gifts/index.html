    Black Friday Men's Grooming Gifts: 4 Picks Worth Watching

    Beauty · November 10, 2026 · 5 min read

    Buying a shaver for someone else is easy to get wrong. A guy with a full beard doesn't want a razor that shaves to skin, a man with swirling neck hair can end up irritated by the wrong head, and a fancy cleaning station is clutter to someone who just rinses under the tap. Here are four picks and who each suits.

    Grooming tools are already part of Amazon's early Black Friday sale. On November 10, Men's Health reported Amazon marking down razors, trimmers and other grooming essentials two weeks ahead of Black Friday, and three of our four picks were among the tools it called out. For a wider list of ideas, start with our Top 10 Men's Grooming Gifts guide.

    Quick picks:

    • Best for the closest daily shave: Braun Series 9 PRO+ (9697cc)
    • Best for curly or multi-direction neck hair: Philips Norelco i9000 Prestige
    • Best for stubble and short beards: Philips Norelco OneBlade 360 Face
    • Best for beard, hair and body in one kit: Philips Norelco All-in-One 7000

    How we chose these

    We started from what deal coverage said Amazon was actually promoting this month: Men's Health's November 10 roundup of Amazon's early Black Friday grooming deals and BladeConcept's Black Friday shaver tracker, last updated November 6. Then we kept one tool per kind of face instead of four similar shavers. We checked each Amazon listing on November 10, when all four were in stock and shipped and sold by Amazon. Every feature below comes from those listings. We haven't tested these ourselves, and we leave prices out because deal prices change daily.

    1. Braun Series 9 PRO+ (Best for the closest daily shave)

    The Braun Series 9 PRO+ Electric Shaver (9697cc) is the pick for a man who shaves clean most days and wants it done in fewer passes. It's the shaver Men's Health named as its best overall electric razor in its November 10 deals roundup. Braun's head uses what it calls 5+1 foil technology: two stainless steel gliding foils, a SkinGuard, separate blades for flat-lying and multi-direction hairs, and a built-in precision trimmer for sideburns and under the nose. A Pro SensoAdapt sensor reads beard density 300 times a second and adjusts power, and Braun says it handles up to a 7-day beard.

    What's in the box: this 9697cc kit includes the 6-in-1 SmartCare Center, which cleans, dries and charges the shaver, plus a cleaning cartridge, a brush, a travel case and a ProComfort pre-shave attachment. It's 100% waterproof for wet or dry shaving, made in Germany, with up to 60 minutes of cordless runtime and up to a 5-year warranty (terms on Braun.com).

    Downsides: The cleaning center needs replacement cartridges over time, which is an ongoing cost, and it takes counter space. A foil head shaves in straight passes, which suits straight-growing beards best. It's also the priciest pick here.

    Who it's for: The daily shaver who wants the most finished result and likes things maintained for him. Skip it if he keeps stubble or a beard; pick #3 or #4 makes more sense.

    2. Philips Norelco i9000 Prestige (Best for curly or multi-direction neck hair)

    Rotary shavers move in small circles, which helps on hair that grows in different directions, especially along the neck. The Philips Norelco i9000 Prestige (Gold Oyster) is Philips' top rotary model, and BladeConcept's tracker listed it as its best rotary deal at Amazon on November 6. Philips lists 360-degree rotating NanoTech blades at 150,000 cutting actions a minute, compact Precision Flex heads for the neck and under the nose, and a sensor that reads hair 500 times a second.

    The useful extra: a pressure light tells him when he's pushing too hard, a common cause of irritation with electric shavers.

    Compared with the Braun: The Braun suits straight growth and a foil-style finish; the i9000 is the better match for curly or coarse hair that swirls. It comes with a pop-up trimmer, charging stand, travel case and cleaning brush, with a 2-year blade lifetime and up to a 5-year warranty.

    Downsides: The power adapter isn't included, only a USB-A cable, so he'll need a wall plug. Rotary shaving can take some getting used to.

    Who it's for: Men with curly growth, neck bumps or sensitive skin who've struggled with foil shavers. Skip it if he's happy with his current foil shaver.

    3. Philips Norelco OneBlade 360 Face (Best for stubble and short beards)

    Not everyone wants a clean shave. The Philips Norelco OneBlade 360 Face trims, edges and shaves with one blade, and Men's Health included it among Amazon's early Black Friday grooming markdowns on November 10. Philips lists a cutter that moves 12,000 times a minute, a 360 blade that flexes with the face, a dual-sided blade for clean edges, and a 5-in-1 comb that sets stubble from 1 to 5 mm. It's IPX7 waterproof for wet or dry use, even in the shower.

    Why it's a smart gift: It keeps stubble at a consistent length with sharp cheek and neck lines. It's also the least expensive pick here, so it works as an add-on gift.

    Downsides: The blade lasts up to about 4 months, so replacement blades are an ongoing purchase. It won't match a foil shaver for a truly close shave, and the comb tops out at 5 mm, so it isn't a full-beard trimmer.

    Who it's for: The stubble or short-beard guy and anyone new to electric grooming. Skip it if he wears a longer beard; pick #4 covers that.

    4. Philips Norelco All-in-One 7000 (Best for beard, hair and body)

    The Philips Norelco All-in-One 7000 22-in-1 Trimmer is the kit for a man who maintains a full beard, buzzes his own hair, or wants one tool for everything. Men's Health listed it among Amazon's early Black Friday trimmer deals on November 10. Philips lists 22 attachments and 28 lengths covering beard, head, body, nose and ears, a precision comb from 1 to 3 mm in 0.2 mm steps, self-sharpening metal blades that need no oil, and a BeardSense feature that boosts power on dense or longer hair. Philips rates it for up to five hours of runtime, with a 5-minute quick charge and a travel lock.

    Downsides: Twenty-two attachments take storage space, and it doesn't shave to skin like picks #1 and #2. It charges over a USB-A cable.

    Who it's for: Bearded guys, home haircut DIYers and dads who'd rather own one trimmer than five. Skip it if he only shaves clean.
