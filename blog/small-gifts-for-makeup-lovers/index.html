    10 Small Gifts for Makeup Lovers They'll Actually Use

    Beauty · October 28, 2026 · 5 min read

    Shopping for someone who loves makeup can feel intimidating. Their vanity is already full, they have opinions about every formula, and a giant palette in the wrong shades can end up unused in a drawer. The good news is that small gifts are often the smartest choice. A great mascara, a lip product they'll reach for daily or a setting spray that keeps their look in place all night gets used up and appreciated.

    This list sticks to small, easy-to-wrap gifts that work for stockings, Secret Santa, coworkers, teens and best friends. Most picks come from our Top 10 Makeup Gifts guide, with a few skincare and nail extras for the makeup lover who also loves a good prep routine.

    How we chose these small makeup gifts

    We focused on products that are small, giftable and mostly under $30, from brands makeup fans already know and trust. We favored things that get used up (mascara, gloss, primer) or upgrade a daily routine (brushes, a cooling roller), because they're low-risk when you don't know someone's exact shade. We relied only on what each product's listing says it does, and we added an honest downside for every pick so you can choose with confidence.

    1. Charlotte Tilbury Airbrush Flawless Setting Spray

    The Charlotte Tilbury Airbrush Flawless Setting Spray is the hero of our makeup gifts guide for a reason. It's an ultra-fine mist that preps and locks makeup in place, and the brand says it helps makeup last for up to 16 hours. It's the finishing step that makes a full face feel worth the effort, and the sleek bottle looks like a luxury gift.

    Downsides: It's the priciest pick on this list, landing under $40 rather than under $30. If they already have a favorite setting spray, they may be loyal to it.

    2. Too Faced Better Than Sex Mascara

    The Too Faced Better Than Sex Mascara is one of the best-known mascaras around. Its hourglass-shaped brush is designed to build dramatic volume and length. Mascara is one of the few makeup products almost everyone uses and replaces often, so it rarely goes to waste.

    Downsides: It's built for drama. Someone who prefers a natural, barely-there lash may find it too bold, and voluminous formulas can take a little practice to avoid clumps.

    3. Rare Beauty Soft Pinch Tinted Lip Oil

    The Rare Beauty Soft Pinch Tinted Lip Oil adds a sheer wash of color and a glossy, non-sticky shine while it nourishes lips. Selena Gomez's brand has a devoted following, so this is an instant win for fans.

    Downsides: It's sheer, so it won't give the payoff of a lipstick. This listing is sold by a third-party seller (it ships from Amazon), so double-check the seller and shade before you buy.

    4. NYX Butter Gloss Lip Gloss 3-Pack

    For a budget-friendly pick, the NYX Butter Gloss 3-Pack delivers creamy, non-sticky shine in wearable nude shades. Three glosses mean they can keep one in a bag, one in the car and one at home. It's an easy gift for teens and adults alike.

    Downsides: Gloss wears off faster than a long-wear lip, so expect touch-ups. Nude shades also look different on different skin tones.

    5. Real Techniques Everyday Essentials Set

    The Real Techniques Everyday Essentials Set pairs soft, cruelty-free face brushes with a Miracle Complexion Sponge for foundation, blush and powder. Brushes are something many makeup lovers forget to replace, so a fresh set feels like a real upgrade.

    Downsides: Someone with a large brush collection may already own these. Brushes and sponges also need regular cleaning, which not everyone enjoys.

    6. e.l.f. Power Grip Primer

    The e.l.f. Power Grip Primer is a cult-favorite gel primer designed to grip makeup so it lasts all day, at a tiny price. It's the kind of product makeup lovers repurchase, so a backup bottle is always welcome.

    Downsides: Primers are personal. The gel texture won't suit everyone's skin, and people with very dry skin may prefer a more hydrating base.

    7. LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask

    The LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask works overnight to leave lips soft and smooth by morning. Smooth lips make every lipstick and gloss look better, which is why this little jar has become a stocking stuffer staple.

    Downsides: It's a nighttime treatment, not a lip color. It comes in a jar, so it's less convenient to apply on the go than a tube.

    8. medicube Collagen Jelly Cream

    Great makeup starts with good skin prep. The medicube Collagen Jelly Cream is a bouncy, jelly-textured moisturizer designed to leave skin looking plump and dewy without feeling heavy. It's the hero of our Top 10 Korean Skincare / K-Beauty guide, which has more picks for glass-skin fans.

    Downsides: Skincare is personal, and anyone with sensitive skin should check the ingredient list first. Some people prefer a richer cream in very cold, dry weather.

    9. Kitsch Cooling Ice Roller for Face

    The Kitsch Cooling Ice Roller goes straight from the freezer to the face to cool, soothe and de-puff tired morning skin before makeup. It's inexpensive, fun to use and pairs with any routine.

    Downsides: It needs freezer space and a few minutes to chill, so it isn't instant. It's a soothing ritual rather than a long-term skincare treatment.

    10. Cuccio Naturale Cuticle Oil

    Makeup lovers tend to love a polished manicure too. The Cuccio Naturale Cuticle Oil hydrates dry, ragged cuticles and helps nails look healthy between manicures. For more ideas, see our nail care and manicure gifts guide.

    Downsides: It's a small, practical gift, so pair it with something more exciting from this list if it's their main present. This listing is sold by a third-party seller.

    Want to go a little bigger?

    If you'd like to splurge on the makeup lover in your life, glow tools are having a moment. The medicube Booster Pro X2 is the hero of our Top 10 Beauty Devices guide. It packs seven glow-care modes into one sleek wand designed to help serums sink in. It's well beyond small-gift territory, so think of it as a main present rather than a stocking stuffer.

    Tips for buying makeup gifts

    • Skip shade-specific products like foundation and concealer unless you know their exact match. Mascara, clear or sheer lip products, primer and setting spray are much safer bets.
    • Check the seller. Several beauty listings on Amazon are sold by third-party sellers. Buying from the brand's official store or Amazon.com is the safest route.
    • Bundle small gifts. Pair a lip oil with the lip mask, or a mascara with the brush set, for a thoughtful mini kit.
    • Think about the routine, not just the makeup. Skin prep and nail care extras make a makeup lover feel pampered.
