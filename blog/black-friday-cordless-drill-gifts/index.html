    Black Friday Cordless Drill Gifts: 4 Picks Worth Watching

    For Him · November 9, 2026 · 5 min read

    A drill is one of the few gifts that gets used the same week it's unwrapped: a shelf goes up, a wobbly chair gets fixed, a TV mount finally happens. The hard part is choosing, because "a good drill" means different things to a first-time homeowner, a weekend DIYer and someone who already has a garage full of yellow batteries. Here are four picks, and exactly who each one suits.

    Power tools have been a big part of Amazon's early Black Friday deals this month, so these listings are worth watching through Cyber Monday. For more deals across categories, see our Black Friday Deals Worth Watching guide.

    Quick picks:

    • Best overall: DEWALT 20V MAX Drill and Impact Driver Kit (DCK240C2)
    • Best budget-friendly first drill: BLACK+DECKER 20V MAX POWERCONNECT Brushless Drill (BLD782D1)
    • Best for someone who already owns DEWALT: DEWALT 20V MAX XR POWERPACK 4Ah Battery 2-Pack
    • Best for small screws and furniture: Fanttik S1 Pro Electric Screwdriver

    How we chose these drills

    We started from what Amazon has actually been promoting in early Black Friday tool coverage this month (Bob Vila, Autoblog and Popular Science deal roundups), then cut the list to tools that cover different kinds of people instead of four similar drills. Every feature below comes from the brands' Amazon listings. We haven't tested these tools ourselves, and we don't quote prices because deal prices change daily.

    1. DEWALT 20V MAX Drill and Impact Driver Kit (Best overall)

    The DEWALT 20V MAX Drill and Impact Driver Kit (DCK240C2) is the kit we'd point most gift buyers to, and it's the DEWALT kit that early Black Friday coverage from Bob Vila and Autoblog called out at Amazon. It pairs two tools: a DCD771 drill/driver for drilling holes and a DCF885 impact driver for driving screws. DEWALT lists up to 300 unit watts out on the drill and up to 1,400 in-lbs of torque on the impact driver, both with LED lights that stay on for 20 seconds after the trigger is released.

    Why two tools matter: Keep a drill bit in the drill and a driver bit in the impact, and you stop swapping bits every time you go from pilot hole to screw. That's the biggest upgrade over a lone drill for decks, cabinets or big furniture builds. The impact driver's 1/4-inch hex chuck takes 1-inch bit tips with one hand.

    What's in the box: both tools, two 20V MAX 1.3Ah batteries, a charger and a contractor bag, backed by DEWALT's 3-year limited warranty.

    Downsides: The included 1.3Ah batteries are compact, not long-running, so long projects mean pausing to charge. Bits aren't included, so add a drill and driver bit set.

    Who it's for: New homeowners and DIYers ready for a "real" kit that can grow, since every DEWALT 20V MAX tool runs on the same batteries. Skip it if they already own a different brand's cordless platform.

    2. BLACK+DECKER 20V MAX POWERCONNECT Brushless Drill (Best budget-friendly first drill)

    The BLACK+DECKER 20V MAX POWERCONNECT Brushless Drill (BLD782D1) is the pick for someone who needs one dependable drill, not a tool system. Popular Science listed this kit among Amazon's early Black Friday tool deals on November 2. BLACK+DECKER lists a brushless motor, 486 in-lbs of max torque, two speed ranges (0-450 and 0-1,900 RPM), 10 clutch positions plus a drill setting, a 1/2-inch keyless metal chuck and an LED light. The kit includes a 2Ah battery with a charge gauge, a charger and a double-ended screwdriver bit.

    Compared with the DEWALT kit: You get one tool instead of two, but a larger single battery and a brushless motor. For pictures, furniture and pilot holes, one well-specced drill is plenty.

    Downsides: There's only one battery, so there's no hot-swap while it charges. The warranty is 2 years standard, and the extra 3 years requires product registration within 30 days. POWERCONNECT is a homeowner-focused platform with fewer heavy-duty tools than DEWALT's.

    Who it's for: College students, first apartments and anyone who drills a few times a month. Skip it if they're planning big remodeling projects; the DEWALT kit is the better start.

    3. DEWALT 20V MAX XR POWERPACK 4Ah Battery 2-Pack (Best for DEWALT owners)

    For someone who already owns DEWALT, the best gift is often more runtime, not another tool. The DEWALT 20V MAX XR POWERPACK 4Ah Battery 2-Pack (DCB2104-2) is the battery Bob Vila flagged as the biggest markdown in its early Black Friday DEWALT roundup. DEWALT says the POWERPACK cells deliver 50% more power and longer life than its traditional cylindrical batteries, with an overmolded, impact-resistant base and an LED charge gauge.

    Why it beats another tool: Starter-kit 1.3Ah batteries drain fast in a hammer drill or saw. Two 4Ah packs mean one can charge while the other works, and they fit every DEWALT 20V MAX tool they already own.

    Downsides: No charger is included, and it's useless outside the DEWALT 20V MAX platform, so confirm the brand in their garage first. When we checked, this listing shipped from Amazon but was sold by a third-party seller, so check who is selling it before you buy.

    Who it's for: Anyone with yellow-and-black tools already. Skip it for beginners who don't own DEWALT yet; start with pick #1.

    4. Fanttik S1 Pro Electric Screwdriver (Best for small screws and furniture)

    Not every job needs a drill. The Fanttik S1 Pro Electric Screwdriver is a pocket-size powered screwdriver for battery covers, outlet plates, computers, faucets and flat-pack furniture, where a full drill risks stripping a small screw. Fanttik lists a 220 RPM motor with three torque settings (1.5, 3 and 4.2 N.m), a 6 N.m max and 8 N.m manual mode for stubborn screws, 16 magnetic S2 steel bits, and a magnetic aluminum case. It charges over USB-C, and Fanttik rates the 2,000mAh battery at about 340 small screws per charge. Watch it in action in Fanttik's S1 Pro TikTok (video: Fanttik, #ad).

    Downsides: It's a screwdriver, not a drill. It won't drill studs or drive deck screws.

    Who it's for: Gadget lovers, PC builders and renters who mostly assemble and fix. Skip it if they need to drill holes; pair it with pick #2 instead. #ad
