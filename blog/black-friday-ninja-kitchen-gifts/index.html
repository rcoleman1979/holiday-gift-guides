    Black Friday Ninja Kitchen Gifts: 4 Picks, Honestly Compared

    Home & Kitchen · November 21, 2026 · 6 min read

    Ninja makes the kitchen gifts people actually ask for by name. The CREAMi turns frozen pints into ice cream, and the SLUSHi makes frozen drinks without ice. The catch is that these are big, specific machines. Buy the wrong one and it lives in a cabinet. Below are four Ninja picks that had a deal on Amazon during its Black Friday Week sale, and who each one really suits.

    If you want smaller or more everyday appliances, start with our Black Friday kitchen appliance gifts post or our handy kitchen appliances guide.

    Quick picks:

    • Best for parties: Ninja SLUSHi XL Smart Frozen Drink Maker (FS601), the big-batch frozen drink machine
    • Best for ice cream lovers: Ninja CREAMi Scoop & Swirl (Amazon exclusive color), scooped ice cream and soft serve in one machine
    • Best for coffee drinkers: Ninja Luxe Café Signature (ES602), espresso, drip coffee and cold brew with a built-in grinder
    • Best for everyday cooking: Ninja Kitchen System Pro+ (BR601AMZ), a blender, food processor and to-go cups in one box

    How we chose these picks

    On November 21 we checked Ninja's CREAMi, SLUSHi, EverSear, Crispi, coffee and blender listings on Amazon. The SLUSHi XL, Luxe Café Signature and Kitchen System Pro+ each showed a "Black Friday Deal" badge, were in stock and were shipped and sold by Amazon. No standard CREAMi or CREAMi Deluxe model showed a deal when we checked. The only CREAMi with one was the Amazon-exclusive Scoop & Swirl, which had a timed deal with a countdown, so treat that one as the most likely to change.

    We skipped the EverSear cookware and the Crispi air fryer because neither showed a deal. We also left out the DualZone air fryer and the Professional Plus blender, which we covered in an earlier post. Details come from the Amazon listings and Ninja's own support pages. We haven't tested these ourselves, and we leave prices out because they change constantly.

    1. Ninja SLUSHi XL Smart Frozen Drink Maker (Best for parties)

    The Ninja SLUSHi XL Smart Frozen Drink Maker (FS601) is the pick for someone who hosts.

    What makes it different: You pour in a liquid (soda, coffee, juice, a margarita mix) and it turns it into slush with no ice and no blending. Ninja lists a 150-ounce capacity (112-ounce max liquid fill), which it says is twice as big as the original SLUSHi FS300. It's also rated twice as fast as the SLUSHi FS301. A feature Ninja calls SlushAssist senses what's inside and adjusts the temperature, and there are six programs in total. Ninja also says it handles higher-alcohol recipes than the FS301. The badged deal covered the Crema color, plus Pink Violet and Green versions when we checked.

    Downsides: It's big. Amazon lists it at about 17 x 9 x 20 inches and 31.5 pounds, so it needs real counter space or a garage shelf. It's a one-job machine: great for parties and summer, easy to forget the rest of the year.

    Who it's for: Someone who hosts game days, pool parties or holidays and has the space. Skip it for a small apartment kitchen or someone who rarely entertains.

    2. Ninja CREAMi Scoop & Swirl, Amazon Exclusive (Best for ice cream lovers)

    The Ninja CREAMi Scoop & Swirl in Bone & Champagne is an Amazon-exclusive color of the NC701 Scoop & Swirl, and it was the only CREAMi we found with a deal.

    What makes it different: Classic CREAMi machines make scoopable pints. This one adds a swirl press, so it can also dispense soft serve. It has 13 one-touch programs, including six soft serve programs (Soft Serve, Fruit Whip, Frozen Custard, CreamiFit, Swirled Frozen Yogurt and Lite Ice Cream). The CreamiFit program is made for lower-calorie, high-protein treats, which is a big reason people want a CREAMi. It comes with two 2-in-1 pints, and Amazon says the pints, lids, paddle and swirl press are dishwasher safe.

    Downsides: A CREAMi isn't instant. Ninja says to freeze your base in the pint for at least 24 hours before processing, on a level shelf, and it doesn't recommend chest freezers. (In a hurry, you can soft-serve store-bought ice cream instead.) This color is a newer listing with few ratings so far (29 when we checked). The deal was a timed one that may already be over, so check before you buy.

    Who it's for: Someone who loves ice cream or soft serve, or a gym-goer who wants high-protein frozen treats and doesn't mind planning a day ahead. Skip it for someone who wants dessert on impulse or has a small, packed freezer.

    3. Ninja Luxe Café Signature (Best for coffee drinkers)

    The Ninja Luxe Café Signature (ES602) is the pick for someone who wants café drinks at home without learning to be a barista.

    What makes it different: It's three machines in one: espresso (single, double or quad shot), drip coffee (classic, rich or over ice) and cold brew (cold-pressed espresso or cold brew coffee). A built-in conical burr grinder has 25 settings. Ninja's Barista Assist guides grinding, weight-based dosing and tamping, and adjusts temperature and pressure for the drink you pick. The Dual Froth System Pro froths milk hands-free, with five presets including cold foam, and the XL milk jug makes enough for two drinks. It ships with the portafilter, three baskets, a tamper and cleaning supplies.

    Downsides: It's a newer model with very few Amazon ratings (15 when we checked), so there isn't much long-term feedback yet. It takes whole beans, so it's not for pod people. An all-in-one machine also means more parts to clean than a basic drip maker.

    Who it's for: A latte or iced coffee fan who wants to stop paying café prices, or a beginner curious about espresso. Skip it for someone happy with a simple drip pot or pods, or a coffee hobbyist who already owns a separate grinder and espresso setup.

    4. Ninja Kitchen System Pro+ (Best for everyday cooking)

    The Ninja Kitchen System Pro+ (BR601AMZ) is the practical pick: the one most likely to get used every week.

    What makes it different: It's a 1,200-watt base with three containers: a 72-ounce pitcher (64-ounce max liquid) with Ninja's Total Crushing blades, an 8-cup food processor bowl that can handle up to 2 pounds of dough, and two 18-ounce single-serve cups with spout lids. There are five preset programs (Smoothie, Frozen, Extract, Chop and Dough), plus low, high and pulse. Compared with the frozen-treat machines above, this one covers smoothies, salsa, chopping and dough in a single box.

    Downsides: It's not exciting to unwrap the way a SLUSHi or CREAMi is. Three containers also take up cabinet space. If they already own a good blender, this is a duplicate.

    Who it's for: Someone setting up a first kitchen, a smoothie-every-morning person, or someone with an old blender on its last legs. Skip it if they already have a solid blender and food processor.

    So, which one?

    Hosts with space: the SLUSHi XL. Ice cream and protein-treat fans who can plan a day ahead: the CREAMi Scoop & Swirl. Coffee lovers: the Luxe Café Signature. When in doubt, the Kitchen System Pro+ is the safest gift, because nearly everyone blends or chops something.
