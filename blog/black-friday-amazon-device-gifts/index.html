    Black Friday Amazon Device Gifts: 4 Picks Worth Watching

    Tech · November 9, 2026 · 5 min read

    Amazon's own devices are some of the easiest gifts to give and some of the easiest to get wrong. A doorbell is useless to someone who rents with no way to mount it, a streaming stick is wasted on a TV that already has apps, and a camera can quietly lock its best features behind a subscription. Here are four Amazon devices, why each is on the list, and who should skip it.

    Deal coverage this week has focused on Amazon's own hardware. On November 9, Tom's Guide reported Amazon taking discounts on Ring doorbells, Blink cameras, Fire TV Sticks and Fire tablets ahead of Black Friday. Deals can change quickly, so check each listing for what's live. For our wider shortlist of devices, start with Penny's Top 10 Black Friday Amazon Devices guide.

    Quick picks:

    • Best for a porch upgrade: Ring Battery Doorbell 2K
    • Best for indoor peace of mind: Blink Mini 2K+
    • Best stocking stuffer: Fire TV Stick 4K Select
    • Best for kids, travel and the couch: Amazon Fire HD 8 tablet

    How we chose these devices

    We began with what press coverage said Amazon was actually promoting on November 9, then kept one device per job so the four picks don't overlap: front-door video, indoor camera, TV streaming and a portable screen. We checked each Amazon listing on November 9 for stock and specs, and every feature below comes from those listings or from Amazon's published specifications. We haven't tested these devices ourselves, and we leave prices out because they change.

    1. Ring Battery Doorbell 2K (Best for a porch upgrade)

    The Ring Battery Doorbell 2K is the pick for anyone who wants to see who's at the door without running wires. Amazon lists Retinal 2K video with a wide-angle view, up to 6x Enhanced Zoom, Live View, two-way talk and real-time motion alerts to your phone. Because it has a built-in battery, you recharge it over USB-C (the cable isn't included) after releasing it from the wall with the included removal tool. It also works with Alexa, so select Echo devices can play custom notifications.

    What makes it different: Compared with a plug-in camera, a battery doorbell goes where the door is, and the built-in battery means no wiring. Compared with the Blink pick below, it's built for the front door, with two-way talk made for a conversation with a delivery driver.

    Downsides: Ring says the history rewind and the AI-powered alerts need a compatible Ring Protect subscription, sold separately. Without one, you mostly get live alerts and Live View. Battery life depends on how busy your doorway is.

    Who it's for: Homeowners and renters who can mount something at the door and want a no-wiring upgrade. Skip it if they live in a building with no door of their own, or if they'd refuse any subscription.

    2. Blink Mini 2K+ (Best for indoor peace of mind)

    The Blink Mini 2K+ is a small plug-in indoor camera, and it's the one we'd choose for a first smart-home gift. Amazon lists 2K video, up to 4x zoom, night vision, improved two-way audio with noise cancellation and motion detection. It can also work as a plug-in chime for a separate Blink Video Doorbell. Place it on a shelf or mount it indoors, and it needs no battery changes because it's powered from an outlet. With Blink's Weather Resistant Power Adapter (sold separately), it can go outside.

    What makes it different: It's the compact option on this list, and it covers a living room, entryway or pet's favorite spot instead of the porch.

    Downsides: It has to stay plugged in, so placement depends on an outlet. Person and vehicle detection requires an optional Blink Subscription Plan, and Amazon lists the cloud storage as a free 30-day trial of that plan, sold separately.

    Who it's for: Pet owners, new parents and people who travel and want to check on the house. Skip it if they want an outdoor camera out of the box, since the outdoor adapter is an extra purchase.

    3. Fire TV Stick 4K Select (Best stocking stuffer)

    The Fire TV Stick 4K Select turns a TV that can't stream into one that can, and it's small enough to fit in a stocking. Amazon lists 4K Ultra HD with HDR10+, Alexa voice search, access to apps such as Prime Video, Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Hulu and Peacock, and the newest Fire TV interface plus Alexa+ search. Setup is plugging it in and connecting to Wi-Fi.

    What makes it different: It's an easy gift that works on any TV with an HDMI port and ends the "which remote" scramble. If you want faster performance, Amazon's pricier Fire TV Stick 4K Plus adds Wi-Fi 6 support and Dolby Vision, according to its listing, while the Select's listing highlights 4K with HDR10+.

    Downsides: Streaming subscriptions are separate. It won't help a TV that already has strong built-in apps.

    Who it's for: College dorms, guest rooms, grandparents and anyone with an older TV. Skip it if their TV already streams well, or if they want Dolby Vision and Wi-Fi 6, in which case look at the 4K Plus.

    View on Amazon

    4. Amazon Fire HD 8 Tablet (Best for kids, travel and the couch)

    The Amazon Fire HD 8 tablet is a portable screen for the people in your life who need one that isn't a phone. Amazon lists an 8-inch HD display, 4GB of RAM, 64GB of storage, a hexa-core processor, a 5MP rear camera, up to 13 hours of use and up to 1TB of expandable storage with a microSD card (sold separately). It also supports Alexa for music, timers and smart-home control.

    What makes it different: Its job is simple media and reading, so the battery and storage matter more than power. If you'd like a version made for children, Penny's guide covers the Fire HD 8 Kids tablet.

    Downsides: It runs Amazon's Appstore, not Google Play, so some apps won't be available. The listing we checked is the lockscreen-ads version, and Amazon sells an ad-free version separately. It's an HD tablet, not a laptop replacement.

    Who it's for: Kids' first tablets (with parental controls), travelers, bedside streaming and ebook readers. Skip it if they need Google apps or serious multitasking.

    Which one should you pick?

    Think about where it will live. The front door gets the Ring doorbell, the living room or nursery gets the Blink Mini 2K+, the TV gets the Fire TV Stick, and the couch gets the tablet.
