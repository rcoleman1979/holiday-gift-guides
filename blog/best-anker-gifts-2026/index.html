The Best Anker Gifts for 2026 (From Someone Who Actually Uses Them)

Tech · October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Some tech brands are a gamble. Anker isn't one of them, at least not in our house. I personally use Anker gear every day, from chargers on the nightstand to power banks in the car, and it's the brand I reach for when I want a tech gift that just works. That's why this one is a little more personal than our usual roundups.

Anker is also bigger than most people realize. Beyond chargers and cables, the family includes Soundcore (speakers and earbuds), eufy (home security) and NEBULA (portable projectors). That makes it easy to find a gift for almost anyone, whether they're a movie lover, a commuter or the person who always has a dead phone. For the full list of ten, see our Top 10 Anker Gifts guide.

How we chose these Anker gifts

We started with the ten products in our Anker guide and grouped them by who they suit best. We leaned on what each product is designed to do according to its listing, picked gear across a wide range of budgets, and called out the honest trade-offs so you can choose with your eyes open. We didn't include anything we'd hesitate to put our own name on.

The star gift: NEBULA Capsule 3 projector

If you only buy one Anker gift this year, make it the NEBULA Capsule 3. It's a soda-can-size 1080p projector with Netflix built in and a rechargeable battery, so it can turn a bedroom wall, a tent or a backyard fence into a big screen without hunting for an outlet.

It's the kind of gift that creates plans: backyard movie nights, sleepovers, camping trips. It suits families, college students and anyone who'd love a big screen without mounting a TV. If movie night is a big deal in their house, our home theater upgrade guide has more ideas.

Downsides: Like any portable projector, it looks best in a dark room, and a tiny body means the built-in sound won't replace a real speaker for big gatherings. (Pair it with the Soundcore speaker below.) Note that this one is sold by Anker's own store on Amazon.

For the music lover: Soundcore speaker and earbuds

The Soundcore Boom 2 is the party speaker of the bunch. It delivers 80W of big, bassy sound, floats in the pool and lasts up to 24 hours, which makes it great for beach days, backyards and tailgates.

For everyday listening, the Soundcore Space A40 earbuds bring adaptive noise cancelling, hi-res sound and long battery life at a fraction of premium-brand prices. They're an easy pick for commuters, students and anyone who's lost one too many earbuds. Browse more ideas in our gifts for music lovers guide.

Downsides: The Boom 2 is built for outdoors and volume, so it's bigger than a pocket speaker. And earbuds are personal: fit varies from ear to ear, so tuck in a gift receipt.

For peace of mind at home: eufy security

Security cameras aren't the most romantic gift, but they're one people end up thanking you for. The eufy SoloCam S340 pairs a 3K wide lens with a zoom lens and solar charging, so there are no wires and no monthly fees for local storage.

At the front door, the eufy Video Doorbell E340 uses two cameras to watch both visitors and packages on the porch, with 8GB of built-in storage and no subscription required. With so many holiday packages arriving, the timing is hard to beat. For more connected-home ideas, see our smart home gifts guide.

Downsides: Both need a little setup with an app and Wi-Fi, and solar charging depends on how much sun the mounting spot gets. Check with renters before gifting a doorbell, since it may need a landlord's OK.

For the always-low-battery friend: chargers and power banks

This is where Anker made its name, and it's still where the brand shines. The Anker 25,000mAh laptop power bank has 165W total output and built-in USB-C cables, so it can charge a laptop, tablet and phone at once. It's a road warrior's best friend and a smart pick for anyone in our travel gifts guide.

For the desk, the Anker Prime 250W charging station fast-charges laptops, tablets and phones from six ports, with a smart display that shows power flow. One tidy hub replaces a tangle of bricks.

Stocking stuffers: small Anker gifts that punch above their size

Not every Anker gift has to be big. The Anker Nano Smart Display charger is a pocket-size 45W USB-C charger with a tiny screen that shows charging speed and temperature. It's a clever stocking stuffer for any iPhone or Android user.

And honestly, the most useful gift on this list might be the simplest: a 6-foot Anker Nano USB-C cable rated for 240W, so it can fast-charge everything from phones to power-hungry laptops. It's the cable everyone secretly needs another of. For more small ideas, see our tech gadget gifts under $50 guide.

Downsides: Chargers are only as fast as the device allows, so an older phone won't suddenly charge at laptop speeds. Make sure the person uses USB-C devices before gifting cables.

The big splurge: Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2

For the camper, the RV owner or the person who worries about power outages, the Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 is a serious backup-power gift. It stores 2,048Wh and pushes 2,400W, enough to run a fridge, power tools or a whole campsite.

Downsides: It's a big-ticket item and it's heavy, so it's best for someone with a clear use in mind, like camping, tailgating or home backup. If you're shopping for a splurge, browse our big-ticket tech gifts over $200 too.
