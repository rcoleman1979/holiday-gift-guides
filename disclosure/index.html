    Disclosure & Privacy

    Affiliate disclosure

    As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

    Pykkd participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. Links to Amazon are affiliate links: if you follow one and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Check the retailer for current prices, availability, specifications and reviews.

    Affiliate links identify our referral to the retailer. The retailer or its network may receive your IP address, browser information and referral details, and may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute purchases. With JavaScript enabled, we ask before opening an affiliate link; declining leaves you on Pykkd. We do not remove referral tags to bypass tracking rules. You can instead find the product independently at the retailer. Our on-site controls cannot change a retailer's practices after you leave.

    We currently use Amazon affiliate links. We have applied to other programs, but an application is not approval. If approved programs are added, we will update these disclosures and assess their tracking requirements before enabling them. We do not currently load Rakuten tracking on this site.

    Privacy policy

    This policy covers Pykkd at pykkd.com. We use information to deliver the website, send newsletters you request, respond to messages and, only if you allow it, measure readership. We do not offer visitor accounts or comments.

    Website hosting and external content

    This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Serving pages necessarily sends connection details such as your IP address and browser information to the host, which may retain technical logs for security and operation. See GitHub's privacy statement. The site uses system fonts rather than loading Google Fonts. External links take you to services governed by their own privacy policies.

    Newsletter and messages

    Merely reading a page or opening our local newsletter panel does not load MailerLite scripts, fonts or form-view tracking. After a notice, you can choose to continue to the MailerLite-hosted signup form. That visit sends normal connection information such as IP address, browser details and referral information to MailerLite and its providers, and may load their scripts, fonts and form cookies/identifiers. Entering and submitting your email there asks to receive the Pykkd newsletter and is separate from analytics choices. The current form uses email confirmation (double opt-in). We do not automatically subscribe you.

    MailerLite processes subscription, confirmation and delivery records and may measure email opens and link clicks using pixels and redirected links. We have not verified that email engagement tracking is disabled, so do not assume it is. Your mail client may let you block remote images. See MailerLite's privacy policy. Unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter; this is separate from turning off website analytics. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of subscription data. Suppression or other records may need to be kept to honor unsubscribe requests or legal requirements.

    If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer and handle your request. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

    Optional analytics

    GoatCounter is blocked unless you enable analytics in . It measures pages viewed, referring sites, browser/device characteristics and approximate location. Requests include your IP address and browser information. GoatCounter describes its analytics as cookieless, with temporary session grouping derived from site, IP address and user agent, held in memory for up to eight hours rather than saving the raw IP address or user agent to disk. This is not a claim that all processing is anonymous or exempt from privacy law. See GoatCounter's privacy information.

    Browser storage and choices

    We store your analytics choice and its date in localStorage under pk_privacy_v1, solely to remember your preference. The choice expires after 180 days; expired or invalid values are removed on the next visit. You can also clear it through your browser. If storage is unavailable, analytics stays off until you choose it for that page. The old newsletter popup and its automatic seven-day prompts have been removed; legacy pk_pop_seen and pk_pop_done values are cleared when our privacy script runs.

    Use the footer's Privacy settings at any time to allow or reject analytics. Withdrawal reloads the page to stop previously loaded analytics code; it cannot undo data already sent. Rejecting does not prevent reading or newsletter signup. Global Privacy Control (GPC) blocks optional analytics and affiliate-link navigation on this site, even if an earlier analytics choice allowed it. To shop without referral tracking, find the product independently. GPC does not unsubscribe you from emails you requested.

    We do not run behavioral-advertising pixels on Pykkd. Affiliate referrals may involve third-party tracking after a click. With JavaScript disabled, analytics remains off and newsletter signup still requires a separate visit and submission; affiliate links remain ordinary outbound links, so do not follow them if you do not want referral tracking.

    Service providers, retention and rights

    GitHub, MailerLite, GoatCounter (if enabled), and retailers you choose to visit process data in accordance with their roles and policies. Processing may take place outside your country. Newsletter information is kept while you remain subscribed and as needed to administer preferences and meet legal obligations; technical records follow the relevant provider's retention settings. We do not promise a fixed analytics retention period that we have not verified.

    Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising. Contact us to exercise a right or appeal a response; we may need information to verify your request. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator. These disclosures and controls are not a certification of legal compliance.
